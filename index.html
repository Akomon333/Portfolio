About

At 16 years old, I met the Prime Minister of Estonia. When I was 15, I met 5+ members of parliament, the president of Estonia, 1+ EU parliament members, many other political figures, and at least one founder of a unicorn.

Won an election for a 2-year term in the Tallinn Youth City Council in 2025, and founded and ran the Lasnamäe IT Club in 2026.

I used to develop games on Roblox 2021–2025, freelanced, found teams, and worked on various projects of mine and others. In total 520K+ visits. Many times my friends and I made games together so we can share our ideas and create something amazing to play.

I spend my free time reading about different technology industries breakthroughs to combine this knoweledge to see full picture and make great decisions regarding strategy/architecture.

I like playing chess/strategy videogames and learning about different niches of tech.

I recently played a VC simulation game where you need to choose startups to invest in based on their potential. As a startup founder it gave me perspective of VCs and learnt more about metrics of a VC fund. Many of my runs ended up on weekly leaderboards.
