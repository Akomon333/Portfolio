About me

Started my tech journey in 2021 making video games on Roblox. My favorite ones were strategy games, but I also coded games in different genres too.

I did coding in Lua and later learned 3D modeling in Blender. I developed video games with my friends for fun and worked on other people's projects too. All the Roblox games I worked on have 520K+ visits in total.

In the summer of 2025 I tried myself in different tech fields. I made a language learning app for myself which would fetch articles from Wikipedia and filter them to show me only words with translations. However, my interest in math and strategy games led me to machine learning. After that I read a lot and made some small ML projects.

In autumn 2025 I ran for and won a 2-year term in Tallinn's Youth City Council. After that I founded and ran the Lasnamäe IT Club from January to May 2026.

In May 2026 I participated in the Estonian Presidential Hackathon on the Numira team and was the team's CTO, leading us to the finals.

In summer 2026 I started working on AkoDB to advance the field of DBMS further.

In my free time I enjoy chess, reading ai papers, and learning more about diffirent aspects of tech.
